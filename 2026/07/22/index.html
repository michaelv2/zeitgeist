Key Themes

This reduces the Sep 30 crystallization event the market was carrying — modestly risk-on, conditional on Senate confirm. But it doesn't touch deficit-driven supply; the long-end overhang simply shifts to the Aug 5 refunding.

A cut through the oil spike would be a genuine dovish shock (buy gold, steepener).
