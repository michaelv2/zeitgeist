Positioning Summary

  • Overweight: energy, defense, gold, TIPS. These are one coherent stagflation-shock book.
  • Underweight: long-duration Treasuries ($ZROZ), consumer discretionary, homebuilders, highest-multiple mega-cap tech.

  • Avoid: fighting the front-end. Do not add duration ahead of the FOMC; the risk skew is hawkish.
