Macro

Rates & Yields — term-premium regime confirmed, advancing

  • Long end broke to a new high through soft data — 10Y at 5.31% (fresh cycle high) with payrolls at +29k. A long end that can't rally on weak jobs is definitively fiscal-supply/term-premium driven, not cycle-driven. Prior thesis intact and strengthening.
  • Steepener is the expression. 10Y–2Y widened to 0.48 (from 0.45); 10Y–3M at 1.06. Stay long front-end / short long-end. The Treasury Quarterly Refunding (early Nov) and FOMC Nov 4–5 balance-sheet language are the near-term supply catalysts.
  • Front end signals pause durability, not cuts. 2Y ~96bp over funds at 3.88%. The spread is positive but below the threshold for clear renewed-hike pricing — the debate is whether the pause holds, not whether the next move is up. This caps duration-UW reversal risk — don't chase the long end as a "recession hedge"; it's trading on supply, not growth.

Inflation — stagflation box intact, Oct 14 is the referee

  • Core CPI +0.29% MoM, headline +0.40%, core PCE +0.25% (Aug) — services-led, untouched by oil's pullback. TIPZ remains the cleanest expression; don't fade TIPS on cheaper crude.
  • One honest caveat: 3-month annualized core is running softer (~2%) than the single Aug print, distorted by the Jun dip. Oct 14 CPI ≥0.3% MoM confirms the box and would reopen the renewed-hike debate; two sub-0.2% prints would open a genuine easing window.

Labor — cooling, not cracking

  • Payrolls +29k Sept, U-rate ticked to 4.2%, but continuing claims actually improved to 1.70M (from ~1.80M in Jul) and JOLTS openings still 7.08M. Growth is softening at the margin without a layoff cycle — which is exactly what keeps the Fed boxed rather than cutting.

Consumer — spending real-positive, sentiment weak

  • Aug retail +1.13% MoM nominal = ~+0.7% real after +0.40% CPI — the second data point against the income-squeeze thesis, even as Michigan sentiment sits at 51.7. Hold discretionary UW pending the next real retail print; one more real-positive beat resolves the squeeze call to the upside.
  • Housing stays the clean UW: 10Y >5.3% keeps mortgage rates punitive; starts fell to 1,275k (Aug) from 1,439k (Jun). Avoid homebuilders regardless of any 2Y relief.
