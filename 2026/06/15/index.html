Key Themes

  • The inflation scare is an energy mirage, and the Iran deal just popped it. Headline CPI ran hot largely on energy while core stayed contained (+0.21% May, core PCE +0.24% April). Nominal retail +0.49% in April was more than offset by +0.64% headline CPI — real spend was slightly negative (~-0.15%), and Michigan sentiment cratered to 49.8 (from 56.6 in Feb). Energy was a meaningful contributor to that squeeze, though the sentiment collapse likely reflects more than gas prices alone; peace at the pump hands real incomes back and is a clear tailwind. Discretionary is priced for the gloom, not the relief — a second-half tailwind the tape is underpricing. Refutes if Michigan breaks <47 or real retail turns negative.

The White House froze Anthropic's top models (Mythos) on an Amazon warning — government is now an active chokepoint on AI deployment/export, a regulatory tail distinct from the capex-durability question, landing on the highest-multiple cohort as Apple licenses Gemini for Siri, one signal of model concentration among a narrowing set of leaders.
