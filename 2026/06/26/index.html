Headline CPI +0.47% is energy-driven and will mechanically ease as oil falls, but the core services stickiness is the problem.
  • Two new supply-side inflation impulses are building (both my inference, not yet in the data): (1) memory-cost goods inflation flowing through Apple/Xbox price hikes; (2) immigrant-labor supply shock from SCOTUS rulings tightening low-wage services/construction wages. Net: I no longer expect clean breakeven compression.
