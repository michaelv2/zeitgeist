Key Themes

  • The bond market is now openly fighting a hawkish Warsh Fed — and the data is on the bond market's side. The FOMC delivered the hawkish surprise the prior memo flagged (2Y 4.05→4.24, 10Y–2Y flattened to 0.27 on Jun 22), but the actual inflation enemy is fading: core CPI was +0.21% MoM in May (after +0.38% in April) and oil has collapsed (WTI $84.65 Jun 15; Brent now <$76 per today's tape). The hawkish event is behind us — that makes post-FOMC the cleaner entry for duration/bull-steepener, not a chase. Confirm: Jul 10 core CPI <0.25%. Kill: ≥0.30%, which vindicates the hike camp.
  • AI/speculative-growth euphoria is taking its first real air out. The global tech rout (Micron, Asia semis, the SpaceX post-IPO de-rate dragging Musk below trillionaire, Tesla lower) intersects the export-control/rare-earth fragmentation overhang into late-July mega-cap earnings. This is a valuation correction and a supply-chain story at once — stay light QQQ. The tell is whether MSFT/GOOGL/AAPL capex guidance holds (durability) or the rout broadens into a multiple reset.
  • Quietly building, under-appreciated: liquidity is ample even as the Fed talks tough. M2 grew +1.1% MoM in May (accelerating) and Chicago Fed financial conditions are very loose (NFCI –0.505, among the loosest readings in recent years). A hawkish Fed into easing financial conditions and falling oil is a soft-landing setup, not a tightening-into-recession one — supportive of risk dips being bought, with the Fed itself as the only real wildcard.

Labor

JOLTS openings jumped to 7,618k (April), muddying the softening read. No recession signal; June payrolls (Jul 2) is the next check.

Consumer

May retail +0.88% MoM nominal, ~+0.41% real even on the hot CPI deflator; that's a second constructive real print. Michigan at 49.8 (April, vs 56.6 Feb) is now contradicted by what households actually spend. Edge toward, not yet a full overweight on, discretionary.

Housing

Play the bill via building products over homebuilders, and only as falling rates (if the duration call is right) become the real catalyst for the group.
