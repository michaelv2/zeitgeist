Key Themes

  • The stagflation-lite nexus is re-arming — and it's not in the actuals yet. WTI has ripped ~12% in two weeks (76.78 Aug 5 → 86.48 Aug 18) on Trump's "toughest-ever" Iran sanctions, Hormuz transits collapsing to single digits, and resurgent Somali piracy — a pure supply-side push landing on a demand side that is slowing. The disinflating prints on file (core PCE +0.13% MoM June ≈1.6% annualized; headline CPI essentially flat in July) are backward-looking and will collide with the energy shock in the Aug CPI (Sept 11). This is the worst mix for a Fed that has held since February — cut the slowing consumer, or defend against a cost-push it can't control. Own the shock (energy, gold), not the victim (discretionary).
  • The consumer is showing real signs of strain — but the floor isn't confirmed broken yet. Walmart — the structural trade-down winner — fell 9% on slowing sales, customers making "trade-offs," and explicit citation of high fuel costs. When the beneficiary of the squeeze is itself squeezed, it corroborates a meaningful softening of consumer demand. July retail sales added to the picture: a ~-0.65% real MoM print that breaks a run of positive months (May +0.46%, June +0.66% real). Together these are enough to keep discretionary UW and avoid staples as a hiding place — but "locked" awaits Aug 26 income/outlays and Sept 15 retail sales. Do not treat staples as a hiding place.
  • AI mega-IPO supply wall is forming. Anthropic guiding to match/top SpaceX's record raise ($65B run-rate), OpenAI's IPO priced by markets as near-lock above $1T (sub-$500B outcome at 0.45%). A wave of primary supply at nosebleed multiples is a quiet overhang on the highest-multiple cohort — the tell for re-rating risk (OpenAI IPO pricing ≥$1T) is approaching but not yet confirmed: the market-implied odds are rising, not the deal itself.

  • Resolution: weight the forward risk over the stale print. A 12% oil move feeds headline CPI and breakevens directly; Aug CPI (Sept 11) is the adjudicator. This argues for covering the prior TIPS UW — an energy-led headline surprise is exactly what TIPS are for.

Rates — Fed boxed in, market pricing no cuts
