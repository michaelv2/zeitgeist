Key Themes

That is term premium building at the long end, not cuts pricing at the short end. A hot CPI today snaps 10Y through 4.75 and duration (ZROZ) takes the pain — the opposite of the cut-driven bull steepening the tape is positioned for. Tell: 2Y sustained <4.20 rebuts this; 2Y ≥4.25 with 10Y >4.75 confirms it.

It "soothes credit markets" today (Bloomberg), but it is leverage layered onto the highest-multiple cohort. Stay barbelled: own memory/HBM and margin-defended moats, underweight the vendor-financed spenders.
