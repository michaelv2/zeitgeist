Daily Memo (18-Sep-2026)

Key Themes

  • The hike landed, and the hidden accelerant is Tokyo, not Washington. Warsh's Fed hiked and said inflation is "still too high"; the same week the BoJ took rates to a 31-year high. The under-appreciated second-order effect: a higher JGB yield repatriates Japanese capital and removes a marginal Treasury buyer just as the US bear-flattens (2Y +40bp in two weeks to 4.74%, 10Y through 5.00%). That is a demand drain into the Nov 5 Treasury refunding — the duration pain is not done. Confirm/refute: 10Y sustained >5% and a soft 30Y auction = thesis live; 10Y back below 4.80% = repatriation fear overdone.
  • The consumer-squeeze call is softened for now — and that's hawkish, not comforting. August retail +1.24% MoM nominal beat CPI (+0.40%) for a clean real-positive rebound, rebutting July's real-negative print. A consumer that won't roll over hands the Fed more cover to stay restrictive. This removes the near-term recession release valve for duration longs — the opposite of what a "weak consumer → cuts" desk expects.
  • Oil is rolling over while core inflation is not. WTI hit $107 (Sep 15) then slipped below $100 on Saudi supply hopes. If sustained, that relieves the consumer but does not rescue duration — core CPI re-accelerated to +0.29% MoM on domestic services, so the sticky leg is now home-grown. Fade the "oil down = Fed pivots" reflex.

Macro

Rates & Yields

  • Hiking-leg risk confirmed; press the duration underweight. 2Y at 4.74% sits well above the (lagged, pre-hike) 3.63% funds print and above the 4.50% hiking-leg threshold. 10Y-2Y flattened to 0.27 from 0.41 — this is bear-flattening on inflation/fiscal, not a growth scare (10Y-3M still positive at 0.82).
  • ZROZ underweight stays the highest-conviction call. 10Y through 5.00% with a Japanese-demand drain and Nov 5 refunding ahead — the term-premium impulse is intact.

Inflation

  • Inflation, not disinflation, is the operative regime. Core CPI +0.29% MoM (Aug), headline +0.40%; M2 still climbing (23,218 in July, ~5% annualized). Aug data largely predates the WTI spike, so any oil passthrough is still ahead even as spot crude eases.
  • Cleaner expressions remain TIPZ and IAU (gold also gets the soft-dollar tailwind, broad USD ~118.2). Next hard reads: Aug core PCE (Sep 30) and Sept CPI (Oct 14).

Labor

  • Labor softening is on the ropes and sidelined for now. Aug payrolls +162k, U-rate 4.1% (cycle low), continuing claims fell to 1,730k (Sep 5), far below the ~1.85M stress line. The labor market gives the Fed substantial cover; treat softening as resolved only if Oct 2 payrolls confirm (second >100k, U-rate ≤4.1%). The hike tail, not a cut, is the live risk.

Consumer

  • Soften the squeeze call: consumer showing resilience, but not yet resolved. August's real-positive retail (+0.84% real) rebuts the one-month July dip, but one print doesn't flip the theme — hold discretionary UW pending a second confirming real-positive (Sept retail). Sentiment recovering (Michigan 55.2 vs 44.8 May low).
  • Split the expression accordingly: hold discretionary UW pending confirmation, keep homebuilders UW. Housing is the clean rate victim — starts fell to 1,275k (Aug) from 1,439k (Jun), Case-Shiller flat and rolling. 5% mortgages-adjacent yields do the damage regardless of consumer spend.
